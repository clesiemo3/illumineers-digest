Hyperia City launches as Quest World Iconic Clash and early set 14 brewing take over

The short version

  • Hyperia City (set 14) went on general release October 23 after prereleases on October 16, adding Coco and the new ink drops mechanic.[1]
  • The Quest World Iconic Clash, a Top 16 single-elimination invitational with Round 1 matches posted all week, is the first big look at the set 14 meta.[2][3][4]
  • Creators are already ranking and brewing Hyperia City decks, including Emerald/Amethyst Retro, Amber/Emerald, Amber/Ruby and Steel builds.[5][6][7][8]

Meta snapshot

  • The 16 QWIC decklists included Amber/Emerald Songs (Bryan Pereira, Jacob Frank, Joseph Quigley), Emerald/Sapphire (Lukas Kaupa), Amber/Steel Rockstar Stitch (vVonderland) and Emerald/Amethyst Retro (Alessandro Cavalli, Fungus). Amethyst/Steel was also in the field.[4]
  • Frank Karsten and Shane Downey played an Emerald/Steel mirror in QWIC Round 1, which the host called extremely technical.[3]
  • Manifest called Flash a new Steel staple and Max Goof one of the most powerful cards in the game. These are opinions from pack-opening videos, not results.[8][9]
  • Podcana asked whether Ruby/Emerald is real after building 15 new decks. The Lore College says Amber/Ruby got an upgrade, and also published a Hyperia City tier list.[5][10][6]
  • Xenulous (Team Maelstrom) took Emerald/Amethyst Retro on Alter Academy.[7]

Standout players

  • QWIC Round 1 pairings posted this week: Fungus vs Ace 103, Frank Karsten vs Shane Downey, vVonderland vs Bryan Pereria, Lukas Kaupa vs Carl Go Boom, and Mo Shamblin vs Pandemonium Heart. Results weren't in the data. The prize is the Hades – Looking for a Deal card.[2][3][11][12][13]

News

  • Hyperia City is out now. Stock was reportedly running low before launch, and Disneyland's Downtown Disney Pin Traders used a virtual queue for launch merchandise.[1][14][15]
  • Illumineer Champions League Season 4 Round 6 ran this week, including ETB vs Triad and World Class Cards vs Manifest, billed as Infinity Week.[16][17]
  • The Cauldron Cup CCQ survey closes 10/30. DLC Tampa 2026 tickets are on sale.[18]

Community pulse

  • Rules of Villainy's episode 119 covers early meta thoughts and Brennan DeCandio's decks. They also ran a Hyperia City Sealed Showdown.[18][19]
  • Manifest, Podcana, Lore College and the Illumiteers (with Team Descent) all covered Hyperia City hype and deck ranking. Lorcana Goons asked whether the set is worth buying in a market watch episode.[20][5][21][22]
  • The Illumiteers posted a 2-case Hyperia City tournament showdown from ROK.[23]
