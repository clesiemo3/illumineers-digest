2026-10-17 – 2026-10-24

Hyperia City goes on general release while Amber/Emerald finishes the old format on top

  • Set 14, Hyperia City, went on general release October 23 after prereleases began October 16. It brings in Pixar's Coco and adds the new one-use ink drops mechanic.
  • The last big pre-Hyperia events (October 10-11) belonged to Amber/Emerald: 3 wins and 8 podium spots across five events, including a full podium sweep at the 142-player CCQ Sevilla.
  • The first look at the Set 14 meta comes from the Quest World Iconic Clash, a 16-player invitational. Amber/Emerald Songs, Emerald/Amethyst Retro, Emerald/Steel and Amber/Steel Rockstar Stitch are all among the 16 decklists.
