2026-10-17 – 2026-10-24

Hyperia City hits shelves, first Set 14 invitational goes to EdLorcana, and Inkdark Collector Boosters get details

  • Set 14, Hyperia City, went on general sale October 23 after prereleases that started October 16. It adds Coco and the new ink drop mechanic, and the release notes change how stacked keywords work and issue errata for Jukebox.
  • Edmond Chiu (EdLorcana) won the 16-player Quest World Iconic Clash invitational on October 17, the first big look at Set 14 decks. His list was titled "Emerald / Steel", and several of the broadcast matches were Emerald/Steel.
  • Into the Inkdark (Set 15) preorders are open for a reported February 12, 2027 release. It brings 12-card all-foil Collector Boosters and the serialized Illustrious rarity (23 copies of each card). Collector Boosters will be sold only at select Play Hub stores and Disney locations.
