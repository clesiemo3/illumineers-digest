2026-10-17 – 2026-10-24

Hyperia City launches as Quest World Iconic Clash and early set 14 brewing take over

  • Hyperia City (set 14) went on general release October 23 after prereleases on October 16, adding Coco and the new ink drops mechanic.
  • The Quest World Iconic Clash, a Top 16 single-elimination invitational with Round 1 matches posted all week, is the first big look at the set 14 meta.
  • Creators are already ranking and brewing Hyperia City decks, including Emerald/Amethyst Retro, Amber/Emerald, Amber/Ruby and Steel builds.
